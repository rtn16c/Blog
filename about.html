About

Return 16 is a blog site I started to write about anything and everything to do with computers and mainframes. Including tutorials, tips and tricks, new things I've learned, and my experiences as a woman in STEM.

Mainframing is for everyone.

I work as a mainframe systems programmer — which means I spend my days (and occasionally my nights) wrestling z/OS and reading a lot of IBM documentation. This blog is part learning log, part reference notebook, part me just yapping into the void. Even when I'm not staring at a green screen, I'm usually still messing around with technology.

Keep in touch with my LinkedIn! I post a lot of events happening in the Melbourne area and Online, and I'm also the Chapter Lead for the Melbourne New to Z Group and an IBM Champion.
